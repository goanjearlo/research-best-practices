# Source Note: Platt et al. (2024) — Managing Collaborative Partnerships in Quantitative Research

**Source:** [Platt 2024 source entry](../sources/platt-2024-managing-collaborative-partnerships.md)

## What this paper is about

This paper picks up where partnership-building leaves off. Instead of focusing on whether to start a partnership (like Huebner et al.), it focuses on how to actually run one well, day to day, once it exists.

## The three phases (main framework)

1. **Initiation Phase** — Confirm why the partnership is needed, get stakeholders aligned, agree on scope, roles, and what success will look like.
2. **Maintenance/Management Phase** — This is the ongoing "running the partnership" phase: ongoing communication, tracking project status, resolving conflicts early, and keeping trust and transparency high.
3. **Evaluation/Sustainability Phase** — Periodically step back, review whether the partnership is actually working, capture lessons learned, and decide whether/how to continue or expand it.

## Why this matters (my interpretation)

A lot of partnerships fail not at the start, but in the middle — communication breaks down, nobody is tracking whether the work is on track, and problems get addressed too late. This framework treats partnership management as an ongoing job, not a one-time agreement.

It also closes the loop that Huebner et al. leaves open: Huebner tells you how to *decide* to build a partnership responsibly; Platt tells you how to *keep it alive* responsibly.
