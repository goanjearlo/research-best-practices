# Source Note: Huebner et al. (2024) — Developing Partnerships for Academic Data Science Units

**Source:** [Huebner 2024 source entry](../sources/huebner-2024-developing-partnerships.md)

## What this paper is about

This paper gives practical guidance for how academic data science / research consulting units ("DSUs") can build partnerships with other departments that actually last, rather than one-off or unstable collaborations.

## The six rules (main framework)

1. **Align with institutional strategic plans** — Make sure partnerships support the university's or institution's broader goals, not just individual requests.
2. **Cultivate partnerships that fit your mission** — Only take on partnerships that match your unit's actual purpose and expertise; avoid drifting into unrelated work.
3. **Ensure sustainability and prepare for growth** — Don't rely only on short-term grant money; plan for the unit's long-term survival and expansion.
4. **Define clear expectations in a partnership agreement** — Put scope, roles, cost, duration, and evaluation criteria in writing (a formal agreement), not just a verbal understanding.
5. **Communicate regularly** — Ongoing, proactive communication is called out as essential to keeping a partnership healthy.
6. **Expect the unexpected** — Build in flexibility for staffing changes, funding shifts, or unplanned disruptions.

## Why this matters (my interpretation)

Without something like this framework, a small team can easily take on too much unaligned work, run out of funding, or have unclear expectations that create conflict later. The six rules act like a checklist for saying yes/no to new partnerships and for setting them up so they don't fall apart.
