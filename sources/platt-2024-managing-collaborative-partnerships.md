**Citation:** Platt, A., Truong, T., Boulos, M., Carlson, N.E., Desai, M., Pomann, G.-M., et al. (2024). A guide to successful management of collaborative partnerships in quantitative research: An illustration of the science of team science. *Stat*, 13(2). https://doi.org/10.1002/sta4.674

## Why I'm using this source

This paper gives a three-phase framework for managing quantitative research partnerships. It pairs well with the Huebner et al. (2024) paper — Huebner covers how to *set up* a partnership; this paper covers how to *run* one day-to-day.

## Summary (based on public abstract sources)

The paper describes three phases for successful collaborative partnerships between quantitative science units and domain research teams:

1. **Initiation Phase** — Establish the need for partnership, align goals, identify stakeholders, define scope, roles, and success measures.
2. **Maintenance/Management Phase** — Ongoing communication, project tracking, conflict resolution, and building trust and transparency.
3. **Evaluation/Sustainability Phase** — Review outcomes, document lessons learned, and plan for maintaining or expanding the partnership.
