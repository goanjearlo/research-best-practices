# Huebner et al. (2024) — Developing Partnerships for Academic Data Science Units

**Citation:** Huebner, M., Bond, L., Stukes, F., Herndon, J., Edwards, D.J., & Pomann, G.-M. (2024). Developing partnerships for academic data science consulting and collaboration units. *Stat*, 13(1), e644. https://doi.org/10.1002/sta4.644

**Link:** https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/sta4.644

**Type:** Peer-reviewed journal article

## Why I'm using this source

This paper gives a practical, six-rule framework for how data science / research consulting units should build and sustain partnerships with other departments. It's a good foundation for a best-practices article on research collaboration.

## Abstract (as published)

Data science consulting and collaboration units (DSUs) serve as core infrastructure for research at universities, offering support in activities such as data management, study design, data analysis, data visualization, predictive modeling, report preparation, manuscript writing, and statistical methods advising. For DSUs to thrive, meaningful partnerships are required. The paper summarizes guidance in six rules: (1) align with institutional strategic plans, (2) cultivate mission-aligned partnerships, (3) prioritize sustainability and plan for growth, (4) set clear expectations through partnership agreements, (5) maintain strong communication, and (6) expect and adapt to the unexpected.
